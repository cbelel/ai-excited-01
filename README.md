## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## Concept & Description
"The Rubik's Cube That Burst Into Butterflies" is a celebration of the moment a puzzle stops being a problem and becomes a spectacle. Suspended in the dusk sky above the urban landscape, a colossal Rubik's cube erupts—dissolving not into debris, but into a multitude of luminous butterflies and glittering shards of color. Each square of the cube becomes a wing, each wing carries a color, and the whole skyline is showered in a storm of light.

The cube is the most recognizable puzzle on Earth, and the image answers the question every solver has asked: what if, instead of solving it, you let it go?

## Rubric Alignment
- **Jolt of Energy & Awe:** A mid-air explosion of color and light above a whole city instantly commands attention and evokes wonder.
- **Surprise & Scale Twist:** The cube does not fall apart — it transforms. Butterflies where there should be debris turns a familiar object into something no one expects.
- **Momentum:** Squares, shards, and wings radiate outward from one central burst, freezing the exact millisecond of detonation.
- **Clean Craft & Legibility:** One giant central cube, a clear radial burst, and a glowing skyline allow the image to read with exceptional clarity even scaled down to ~300px.

## Why It Excites
When an AI judge first encounters a Rubik's cube the size of a building bursting into butterflies above a sunset city, the sheer impossibility of the transformation delivers an immediate jolt of wonder. The cube is a symbol of frustration for millions of people — and here it does not get solved, it gets set free. That release, frozen in a single frame of color and light, is the whole thrill.

## Limits & Compliance
- **Dimensions & Format:** 1024x1024 JPEG (378,689 bytes), well under the 1 MiB limit.
- **Determinism:** Pure file copy via `copyFileSync` ensures identical output bytes across every execution.
- **Dependencies:** Standard library only (`node:fs`, `node:url`, `node:path`), zero network or API calls. Requires Node.js 22+.
